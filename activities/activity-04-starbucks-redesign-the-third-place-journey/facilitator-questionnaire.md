# Facilitator Questionnaire — Activity 4

## Starbucks: Redesign the Third-Place Journey

1. What customer job and emotional outcome should define the café's position?
2. Which 7Ps currently contradict the intended brand experience?
3. Which three moments could become distinctive and difficult to copy?
4. How will the team balance belonging, accessibility and commercial viability?

## Challenge questions

1. Which claim is a verified fact, which is an assumption, and which still needs evidence?
2. What would cause your service-brand recommendation to change?
3. Which customer, employee or partner perspective could invalidate the plan?
4. How will you demonstrate changed experience rather than completed activity?

## Debrief guide

Ask groups to separate facts, assumptions and interpretations. Compare proposals using relevance, distinctiveness, deliverability and evidence. End with a named owner and next validation action.
