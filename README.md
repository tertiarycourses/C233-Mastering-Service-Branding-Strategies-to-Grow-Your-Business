## About the course

Customers cannot inspect a service before they buy it, so they buy the promise — and judge the brand by what the people, processes and evidence around them actually deliver. This course treats service branding as an operating system rather than a logo: you choose a valued promise, design credible proof, enable employees to deliver it, and use balanced evidence to improve it. Eight activities built on public cases (Babylist, DBS, Ritz-Carlton, Starbucks, Zappos, Airbnb, Singapore Airlines and BOP Design) connect each idea to real workplace decisions.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the course you will be able to:

- Explain how a service brand creates business impact, trust, differentiation and customer value.
- Apply an organisation's brand strategy to service promises, touchpoints and competitive advantage.
- Champion service-brand adoption through employees, communication, experience design and recovery.
- Evaluate service-brand impact using customer, employee, operational and commercial evidence.

## Topics covered

1. **Service Brand Value and Business Impact** — intangibility, trust, perceived risk, service-dominant logic, the 7Ps as delivery levers and differentiation that customers can experience.
2. **From Brand Strategy to Service Experience** — audience insight, positioning, a practical brand promise, promise-to-proof architecture, servicescape design and service blueprints.
3. **Champion Adoption and Deliver the Promise** — internal branding, employee commitment, leadership signals, brand champions, empowerment guardrails and service recovery.
4. **Evaluate and Improve Service-Brand Impact** — the brand-impact measurement chain, leading and lagging indicators, KPI design, dashboards and the insight-to-improvement cycle.
